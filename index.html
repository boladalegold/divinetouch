Services

Support for the season you’re in.

Whether you are in a hard chapter or looking for a steadier path, therapy gives you room to be heard and practical tools you can use in daily life.

Adults

Individual therapy

A private, steady space to understand what you are carrying and build tools to move forward.

  • Anxiety, depression, and mood
  • Trauma, grief, and loss
  • Stress, burnout, and work-related strain
  • Life transitions and self-worth
Partners and spouses

Couples therapy

We begin by getting clear on why you’ve come: to make a hard decision about the relationship, or to understand and repair what has been broken. That answer shapes the work.

  • Communication and recurring conflict
  • Rebuilding trust and connection
  • Stress on the marriage from parenting, illness, or loss
  • Clarity at a crossroads
Children, teens, and their families

Children, teens & families

Support for young people and the adults who love them, with parents included as partners in the work.

  • Teen anxiety, depression, and withdrawal
  • Behavior, emotions, and school stress
  • Family conflict and communication
  • Parenting a child who is struggling
Personal growth

Mentoring & growth

Guidance for resilience, healthier patterns, and personal growth, for people who want structured support beyond a crisis.

  • Goal setting and accountability
  • Healthy boundaries and habits
  • Purpose and next steps

Mentoring is a non-clinical service and is not a substitute for therapy or diagnosis.

Compassionate

About your clinician

Meet Ms. Boladale, LCPC

Ms. Boladale is a Licensed Clinical Professional Counselor who has worked in behavioral health for more than fifteen years. Her career began with adolescents in residential treatment, where she saw how deeply a family’s wellbeing shapes a young person’s healing. That experience still guides her work with individuals, couples, and families today.

She founded Divine Touch Therapeutic Counseling & Mentoring Services in 2018 to offer care that is clinically sound, deeply respectful, and open to the whole person, including faith for those who want it.

LicenseLicensed Clinical Professional Counselor (LCPC), Maryland
EducationMA, Counseling Psychology, Bowie State University
ExperienceAdolescent residential treatment, community mental health, private practice
FocusAnxiety, depression, trauma, relationships, and family concerns

Ms. Boladale is also an author, including Anchoring the Storm, a guide for parents of teens navigating anxiety and depression.

Questions

Common questions

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Yes. Sessions are available in person at our Glen Burnie office or by secure video for clients located anywhere in Maryland.

Who do you work with?

Children, teens, adults, couples, and families. During your consultation call we’ll make sure our services are a good fit for what you need.

Do I have to share your faith to work with you?

No. Faith is welcome here for clients who want it, and never required. Your care is shaped around your goals and values.

How do I schedule my first appointment?

Call 410-696-3335 or email admin@dttcams.com with your name and best contact number. The office will reach out to schedule a brief consultation and send secure intake paperwork.

What if I’m in crisis?

This office is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 911. In the United States you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline at any time.
